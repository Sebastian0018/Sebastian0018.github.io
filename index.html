The pivot

I know how the work happens on site. Now I'm learning the software side.

In Colombia I ran the day-to-day of construction work: schedules, budgets, subcontractors, crews and a lot of progress reports. After moving to Boston in 2022, I decided I wanted to work on the tools behind that kind of work, not just use them.

I'm a beginner on that side. I'm learning Python from scratch and practicing with AI tools; this website is one of my first projects, made with Claude Code.
