From job sites to learning code.

Civil engineer from Bogotá, living in Boston since 2022. I speak Spanish and English, and I usually have a film camera with me.

Bio

I'm David. I studied civil engineering at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana in Bogotá, where I specialized in hydro-technics. Our project in that area earned an honorable mention, which I'm still a little proud of.

After graduating I went straight to site work. I coordinated hydrosanitary and gas designs at Hidrarco, supervised part of the Banco de la República remodel (the vault included), and managed the construction of Farmatodo stores in Medellín, Santa Marta and Montería. Most of what I know about how buildings really get built, I learned there, not in a classroom.

In 2022 I moved to Boston, and since 2023 I've worked at Rochambeau, a busy French brasserie. Different field, similar lessons: keep calm when things get hectic, and look after the small details before they turn into big ones.

Outside work I shoot film, mostly buildings, bridges and streets. Photography has been a great teacher. With a limited roll you learn to be patient, to wait for the light, and to notice things most people walk past. Being observant helps on a job site too.

These days I'm learning Python from scratch and figuring out how to build things with AI tools. This website is one of my first experiments; I made it with Claude Code. I'm a beginner on the software side, but I know the construction side well, and I'd like to work where the two meet.
