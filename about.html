Bio

I'm David Sebastian Becerra Davila, a civil engineer from Pontificia Universidad Javeriana, where I specialized in hydro-technics and received an honorable mention for our hydro-technics project.

My first engineering years were hands-on: coordinating hydrosanitary and gas design at Hidrarco, supervising critical areas of the Banco de la República remodel — including the secure vault — and managing construction of Farmatodo pharmacy stores in three Colombian cities. Most of what I know about how buildings actually get built, I learned on site.

I moved to Boston in 2022, and since 2023 I've worked front-of-house at Rochambeau, a busy French brasserie. It's a different field, but the same fundamentals: coordination, pressure, and systems that either help people or get in their way.

I want to work on the software side of construction. I'm honest about where I am: learning Python from the basics, and learning to build with AI tools — this website was designed and coded with Claude Code as part of that. The site experience is real; the software skills are in progress.
