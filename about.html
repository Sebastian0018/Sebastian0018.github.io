Bio

I'm David Sebastian Becerra Davila — a Civil Engineer with hands-on experience managing construction projects, from pharmacy store buildouts across three Colombian cities to the remodel of a national bank's secure vault. I trained as an engineer, but most of what I know about how buildings actually get built I learned standing on the site, not in a classroom.

I moved to Boston in 2022. The relocation meant restarting professionally — working front-of-house at Rochambeau, a $10M/year French brasserie, while figuring out my next move. Instead of treating that as a detour, I used it as an observation post: I watched how a demanding operation runs on people, coordination, and improvised workarounds, and it clarified something I'd already suspected from the construction side — the software the industry uses doesn't reflect how the work actually happens.

So I made a deliberate decision: learn to build that software layer myself, instead of waiting for someone else to. I'm currently learning Python, studying AI applications, and building early tools — like a WhatsApp automation bot — at the intersection of construction operations and technology. The construction management experience isn't background for this pivot; it's the edge that makes it credible. Most people building ConTech software have never run a job site. I have.
